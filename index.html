✦ SMARTS — Apply this test to every Key Result
S
Specific Names the programme, geography, beneficiary count, or exact scope — no vague language e.g. "in Gumla district" not "in target areas"
M
Measurable Has a number, %, ₹ figure, or count that a third party can verify — not "improved" or "better" e.g. "90% data completeness" not "good data quality"
A
Ambitious Pushes the team beyond last cycle's baseline — not a repeat of business-as-usual e.g. "reduce attrition from 22% to 14%" not "maintain attrition levels"
R
Result-oriented Describes what will be achieved — not just what will be done or delivered e.g. "70% of youth placed in jobs" not "run placement drives"
T
Time-bound States a specific month and year — not "by end of year" or "soon" e.g. "by March 2026" not "by the end of the cycle"
S
Stretching Challenges the team to innovate or solve something they haven't solved before — not just doing more of the same e.g. "first-of-its-kind government MoU" not "continue existing programme"
